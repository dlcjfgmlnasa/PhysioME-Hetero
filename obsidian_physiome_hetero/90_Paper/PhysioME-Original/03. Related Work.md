## 일반적으로 다룰 4개 갈래

### 1. Self-Supervised Learning for Biosignals

- Time-series SSL: SimCLR-TS, TS2Vec, TST, T-Loss
- Biosignal-specific: NeuroNet, BIOT, ContrastiveECG
- Masked autoencoder for waveforms: MAE-EEG, AT-MAE

### 2. Multimodal Representation Learning

- Vision-language: CLIP, ALBEF, BLIP
- Audio-visual: AVS-MAE, MAViL
- Biosignal multimodal: PhysioNet 챌린지 기반 이전 모델들

### 3. Missing Modality Robustness

- Cross-modal generation / imputation: VAE 계열, GAN 계열
- Modality dropout (Hussen Abdelaziz et al.)
- Missing-modality MAE 변종

### 4. Clinical Applications

- Intra-operative hypotension prediction
- 멀티모달 → unimodal 비교 연구들
